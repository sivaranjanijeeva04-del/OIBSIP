HIS STORY

About Him

A life dedicated to science, education and the nation.

1931 – 2015

A Dreamer Who Inspired a Nation

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist who served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. He was widely loved for his humility, wisdom and dedication to young people.

Beyond science and public service, he encouraged students to think creatively, work sincerely and contribute to the development of India.

"Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."

THE MILESTONES

His Remarkable Journey

Important moments that shaped his extraordinary life.

1931

A Humble Beginning

Born on 15 October in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a family that valued hard work and simplicity.

1950s

Education in Aeronautics

Studied aerospace engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology.

1960s

Scientific Career

Began working with India's defence and space research programmes, contributing to important aerospace projects.

1980

Satellite Launch Success

The SLV-III mission successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit.

1997

Bharat Ratna

Received India's highest civilian award in recognition of his contributions to the nation.

2002–2007

President of India

Served as the 11th President of India and became widely known as the People's President.

2015

A Lasting Legacy

Passed away on 27 July while delivering a lecture to students at IIM Shillong. His legacy continues to inspire generations.
